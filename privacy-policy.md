# Block Booster Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Block Booster is designed as an offline puzzle game. Version 1.0.0 does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers.

The game stores local gameplay information such as the high score on the device. This information remains on the device unless the user clears app data or uninstalls the app.

The app may request vibration access solely to provide gameplay feedback. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS access is required.

If advertising or analytics SDKs are added in a future release, this policy will be updated before that release to describe the data collected and the relevant third parties.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
